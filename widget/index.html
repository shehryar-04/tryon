🔍 The Kurta-to-Shirt Cross-Category Algorithm in Action:

When shoppers upload a photo wearing a Kurta (which reaches to the knees) and select a Shirt (which ends at the waist), the platform activates Stage 2 Delta Processing. Instead of leaving ghost flaps hanging over the knees, the system segments the full kurta with Grounded-SAM 2, removes the region below the waist, reconstructs the trousers using LaMa inpainting, and hands a clean agnostic canvas to the Leffa diffusion engine.
